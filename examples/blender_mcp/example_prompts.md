# Example Prompts for Blender MCP + Bedrock Agent

Here are some example prompts you can use with the Blender MCP + Bedrock Agent integration:

## Basic Scene Creation

- "Create a simple scene with a cube at the center and a sphere 2 units to the right"
- "Add a plane below the objects to serve as a ground"
- "Create a low-poly tree with a brown trunk and green leaves"

## Material and Appearance

- "Make the cube red and metallic"
- "Apply a glass material to the sphere with a slight blue tint"
- "Create a checkerboard pattern on the ground plane"
- "Make all objects in the scene have a cartoon-like appearance"

## Lighting and Environment

- "Set up three-point lighting for the scene"
- "Add an HDRI environment map for realistic lighting"
- "Create a spotlight shining on the main object"
- "Make the lighting look like sunset"

## Camera and Rendering

- "Position the camera to view all objects from a 45-degree angle"
- "Set up the camera for an isometric view"
- "Configure render settings for a quick preview render"
- "Create an animation that rotates the camera around the scene"

## Scene Inspection and Modification

- "Get information about all objects in the scene"
- "Select all objects with a red material and move them up by 1 unit"
- "Scale the selected object to be twice as large"
- "Rotate the cube 45 degrees around the Y axis"

## Using Poly Haven Assets

- "Download and add a rock model from Poly Haven"
- "Apply a wood texture from Poly Haven to the cube"
- "Set up an outdoor HDRI environment from Poly Haven"
- "Create a scene with Poly Haven assets that looks like a forest"

## Using Hyper3D Rodin

- "Generate a 3D model of a garden gnome through Hyper3D"
- "Create a detailed dragon model using Hyper3D"
- "Generate a 3D model of a futuristic car and place it in the scene"

## Advanced Operations

- "Write a Python function that creates a spiral of cubes"
- "Create a procedural landscape with mountains and valleys"
- "Set up a physics simulation with the sphere falling onto the plane"
- "Export the current scene as a glTF file"

## Complex Scene Creation

- "Create a low poly scene of a medieval village with houses, a well, and some trees"
- "Make a simple solar system with planets orbiting around a sun"
- "Create an underwater scene with fish, coral, and bubbles"
- "Build a simple room interior with furniture and proper lighting"
